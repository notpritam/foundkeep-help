title: An inspiration board

**Who it's for:** designers, writers, makers, and anyone who collects visual
references — screenshots of good UI, photos, tweets with a sharp turn of phrase.

A folder of screenshots you never open isn't inspiration; it's clutter. FoundKeep
keeps the images *and* makes them findable, because it can read the text inside
them.

## Set it up

1. Make a **folder** or a [collection](/collections/) — "UI inspiration",
   "Colour", "Copy I love".
2. On your phone, add FoundKeep to the **Share** sheet; on desktop, pin the
   [extension](/save-from-anywhere/).
3. Turn on [AI processing](/privacy/) so images get OCR and tags.

## The workflow

1. **Grab it fast.** Screenshot on your phone, then Share → FoundKeep. On desktop,
   right-click an image and save it, or save a tweet whole.
2. **Search by what's in the picture.** OCR pulls the words out of screenshots, so
   searching "pricing table" finds the screenshot of one — no captioning by hand.
   See [Search & filters](/search-and-filters/).
3. **Tag the vibe.** Add personal tags like `bold-type`, `dark-ui`, `warm-palette`
   so you can pull a mood together later. See [Folders & tags](/folders-and-tags/).
4. **Link the set.** When a few references share a direction, [link them](/link-saves-and-graph/)
   so opening one leads to the rest.

## Share it

Turn the folder into a [collection](/collections/) and share it with a
collaborator as a member, or make it public so others can follow your board. See
[Collections & sharing](/collections/).
