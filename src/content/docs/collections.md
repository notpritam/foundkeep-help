A **collection** is a group of saves you can share. Where folders and tags keep
*your* library organized, collections are how a set of saves becomes something
other people can see, follow, or help build.

## Create a collection

1. From your [dashboard](https://foundkeep.app/dashboard), create a new collection
   and give it a name.
2. Add saves to it. A save can be in a collection and still live in its own folder
   — collections don't move things, they gather them.
3. Keep it private, or share it when it's ready.

## Members vs. followers

Collections have two kinds of people, and the difference matters:

- **Members** can contribute. Invite someone as a member and they can add saves to
  the collection and help shape it. Use members for a shared, collaborative
  collection.
- **Followers** can view. When you make a collection public, people can **follow**
  it to keep up with what you add, without being able to change it.

In short: **members build it, followers watch it.**

## Sharing and public collections

- Share a collection privately with the people you invite as members.
- Or make it **public** so anyone can discover and follow it.
- Public collections show up in **discovery**, where you can find and follow
  collections other people have made public.

You're always in control of what's shared — everything in FoundKeep is
[private by default](/privacy/), and a collection is only visible to others once
you choose to share or publish it.

## A note on moderation

The owner of a collection moderates it — you decide what stays. There is no
public "report" button today; moderation is owner-run.
