**Who it's for:** anyone whose "read later" is really "read never" — a graveyard of
tabs, bookmarks, and links texted to yourself.

Most reading lists fail because saving is easy and *finding your way back* is hard.
FoundKeep fixes the second half: one place, searchable, with a simple habit for
clearing it.

## Set it up

1. Install the [browser extension](/save-from-anywhere/) and the mobile app so you
   can save from your laptop *and* your phone.
2. Decide on one **personal tag** for the list — `read-later` works well. See
   [Folders & tags](/folders-and-tags/).
3. Turn on [AI processing](/privacy/) so each article arrives with a short summary.

## The workflow

1. **Save in one motion.** See something worth reading? Click the extension, or
   Share → FoundKeep on your phone. Add the `read-later` tag.
2. **Triage with summaries.** When you've got a few minutes, open your list and
   read the AI summaries first. Some articles you can retire on the summary alone.
3. **Filter to your list.** Search or filter by `read-later` to see only what's
   waiting. Filter by **type: bookmark** to focus on articles.
4. **Clear as you go.** Read it, then remove the `read-later` tag (or add a `read`
   tag). The list shrinks, which is the whole point.

## Why it sticks

- Saved articles keep a **readable copy** of the text, so a paywall or a dead link
  later doesn't cost you the piece.
- Everything is searchable, so even things you never "finished" are still findable
  the day you actually need them. See [Search & filters](/search-and-filters/).
