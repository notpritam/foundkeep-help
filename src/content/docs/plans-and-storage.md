FoundKeep has two plans. Both let you save, organize, search, and connect your
agents — the difference is mostly about how much you can keep.

## Free

The Free plan is a real, useful FoundKeep account. You can save from every
surface, organize with folders and tags, search your whole library, build
[collections](/collections/), and [connect Claude & Codex](/connect-claude-codex/).

## Pro — $5/month

Pro is for people who lean on FoundKeep every day and want more room to keep
things. It's **$5/month**.

## AI during early access

[AI processing](/privacy/) — summaries, tags, categories, OCR, and readable-text
extraction — is **available on all plans during early access**, Free included.
It's consent-gated: you turn it on in settings, and you can re-run it per item.
This is what makes your library searchable, so it's worth enabling.

## Friends beta

If you're here through the **friends beta**, your account has **complimentary Pro**.
You don't need to do anything to claim it.

## Storage cap

Every account has a **storage cap** — a limit on how much you can keep. Preserved
articles and tweets, images, files, and other media all count toward it. Pro
raises the ceiling. If you're bumping against the cap, that's the signal to
consider Pro.
