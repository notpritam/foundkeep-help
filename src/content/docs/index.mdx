FoundKeep is a private place to keep the things worth keeping. Save a link,
a highlight, a photo, a file, a tweet or thread, or a quick note — from your
browser, your phone, or any app's share menu — and FoundKeep keeps it and helps
you find it again. It's private by default; you decide what, if anything, to share.

## How it works

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="1. Save it" icon="add-document">
		Save from the browser extension, your phone's Share sheet, or the web
		dashboard. Bookmarks, highlights, images, files, tweets, and notes all land
		in one place.
	</Card>
	<Card title="2. It gets organized" icon="list-format">
		FoundKeep can summarize, tag, categorize, and read the text inside what you
		save — so everything becomes searchable. You add your own folders and tags on
		top.
	</Card>
	<Card title="3. Find it again" icon="magnifier">
		Full-text search, filters, links between related saves, and a graph of
		connections mean you can actually get back to what you kept.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
