FoundKeep is designed to catch things wherever you find them. There are four
ways in, and they all land in the same library.

<CaptureSurfaces />

## What you can save

FoundKeep handles many kinds of things, and it keeps the right part of each:

- **Notes** — quick thoughts you type yourself.
- **Bookmarks** — a link, saved with its title and a readable copy of the article.
- **Tweets and threads** — the full text and media, preserved (not just a link).
- **Selections (highlights)** — a passage you highlight on a page.
- **Screenshots and images** — with text pulled out so they're searchable.
- **Video, audio, and documents** — kept as originals.
- **Files** — anything else you want to hold onto.

## The four ways in

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Browser" icon="puzzle">

The extension is the fastest way to save while you read.

<Steps>

1. Install it from [foundkeep.app/beta](https://foundkeep.app/beta).
2. **Pin it** so the FoundKeep icon sits in your toolbar.
3. To save the page you're on, click the FoundKeep icon.
4. To save a link, image, or a highlighted passage without leaving the page,
   select it, **right-click**, and choose FoundKeep from the context menu.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Saved articles keep a readable copy of the text, so the page is still useful even
if the original changes or disappears. See
[Keep tweets forever](/usecase-keep-tweets-forever/) for how preservation works
on tweets and threads.
</Aside>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iOS" icon="apple">

<Steps>

1. Install the FoundKeep app from the App Store.
2. In any app — Safari, Photos, a PDF, another app — tap **Share**.
3. Choose **FoundKeep** from the share sheet.
4. Add a note or a tag if you want, then save.

</Steps>

You can also open the app and **write notes** directly when you just want to
capture a thought.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android" icon="mobile-android">

Android is in beta as a signed APK.

<Steps>

1. On your phone, open [foundkeep.app/beta](https://foundkeep.app/beta) and install
   the APK. (You may need to allow installs from your browser once.)
2. In any app, tap **Share**.
3. Choose **FoundKeep**, add anything you like, and save.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Web" icon="window">

No extension or app required — you can save straight from
[foundkeep.app/dashboard](https://foundkeep.app/dashboard):

<Steps>

1. **Paste a URL** into the add box to save a link or article.
2. **Drop files** onto the page — images, PDFs, audio, video, or documents.
3. **Write a note** when you just want to keep a thought.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## After you save

However it arrives, a new save can be summarized, tagged, categorized, and made
searchable automatically — see [Folders & tags](/folders-and-tags/) and
[Search & filters](/search-and-filters/).

<Aside type="note" title="Private by default">
You stay in control of what's shared; everything is private by default. See
[Privacy](/privacy/).
</Aside>
