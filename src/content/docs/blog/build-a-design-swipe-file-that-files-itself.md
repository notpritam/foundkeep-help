Every designer and builder ends up with a hoard: screenshots of good UI,
gradients you'll use *someday*, a running list of component libraries, whole apps
you saved because one screen was perfect. The hoard is fine. The problem is that a
folder of 400 screenshots you never open isn't a swipe file — it's a drawer you're
afraid to look in.

The fix isn't more discipline. It's making the pile file and find *itself*.

## Clip it wherever you find it

Good references show up mid-scroll, and the tool that catches them has to be right
there. FoundKeep gives you a few ways in, all landing in one library:

- The **browser extension** (Chrome, Edge, Brave) — click the icon to save the
  page, or right-click an image or a highlighted passage to save just that.
- Your **phone's Share sheet** — screenshot something, tap **Share**, choose
  **FoundKeep**. iOS is on the App Store; Android is a signed APK at
  [foundkeep.app/beta](https://foundkeep.app/beta).
- The **web dashboard** — paste a URL or drop image files straight in.

The full rundown of every way in is in [Save from anywhere](/save-from-anywhere/).

## The part that makes it a swipe file, not a drawer

Here's the difference. When you turn on AI processing, FoundKeep runs **OCR** on
your images — it pulls the words *out* of the picture. So a screenshot of a
pricing page is findable by searching `pricing`. A screenshot of a settings screen
turns up when you search for a label that was in it. You don't caption anything by
hand; the text was already there, and now it's searchable. Alongside OCR each save
gets a short **summary** and **tags**, so even a wordless gradient lands with
something to grab onto later. See [Search & filters](/search-and-filters/) for
what search actually reaches.

That's what flips a hoard into a resource: you stop *remembering where you put it*
and start *asking for what's in it*.

## Keep it tidy without babysitting it

Group the visual stuff in a **Design folder**, or turn a set into a
[collection](/collections/) you can share. Add **personal tags** for the vibe —
`bold-type`, `dark-ui`, `warm-palette` — so you can pull a mood together in one
go. And if you've connected an assistant, let it keep the folder in order: filing
new clips, tagging them, and linking a gradient to the layout it would suit. (It
can tidy and link, but it can't delete — that stays with you.)

For the full setup, [An inspiration board](/usecase-inspiration-board/) walks
through building one end to end.

## How to do this in FoundKeep

1. Make a **Design** folder — or a [collection](/collections/) if you want to
   share it.
2. Pin the [browser extension](/save-from-anywhere/) and add FoundKeep to your
   phone's Share sheet.
3. Turn on **AI processing** so screenshots get OCR, a summary, and tags.
4. Clip freely — screenshots, gradients, component-library links, whole app
   screens.
5. Find them by what's *in* them: search a word from a screenshot, or filter to
   **type: image**. See [Search & filters](/search-and-filters/).
6. Add personal tags for the vibe, and [link](/link-saves-and-graph/) references
   that share a direction so they surface together.
