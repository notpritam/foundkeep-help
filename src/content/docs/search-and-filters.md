Saving is only half of FoundKeep. The other half is getting back to what you
kept. Search and filters are how you do that.

## Full-text search

Search looks across everything FoundKeep knows about a save, not just its title:

- **Titles** of pages, files, and notes.
- **Your notes** and the text you wrote.
- **AI summaries** generated for each save.
- **Text pulled out of images** with OCR — so a screenshot of a receipt or a slide
  is findable by the words inside it.
- **Readable text** extracted from saved articles.

Because summaries, OCR, and extracted text feed search, turning on
[AI processing](/privacy/) makes your whole library dramatically easier to
find your way around. It's what turns a pile of saves into something you can
actually query.

To search:

1. From your [dashboard](https://foundkeep.app/dashboard), start typing in the
   search box.
2. Results update as you go, ranked across all the text above.
3. Open a result, or keep typing to narrow it.

## Filters

When you know roughly what you're after, filters cut the library down fast:

- **Type** — note, bookmark, tweet, selection, screenshot, image, video, audio,
  document, or file.
- **Status** — where a save is in its life, including whether it's still being
  processed.
- **Category** — the category FoundKeep assigned during processing.

Combine filters with a search term to zero in: filter to **images**, then search
for a word that appears in one — OCR does the rest.

## Tips

- Searching by a **personal tag** is the most reliable way back to things you
  meant to revisit. See [Folders & tags](/folders-and-tags/).
- If a save isn't turning up, check that AI processing ran on it — you can
  [re-run processing](/tips-and-shortcuts/) on any single item.
- Filter by **type: tweet** to browse everything you've preserved from X. See
  [Keep tweets forever](/usecase-keep-tweets-forever/).
