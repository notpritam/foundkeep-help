FoundKeep is a private place to keep the things worth keeping and find them
again. This page gets you from zero to your first save in a couple of minutes.
The deeper guides are linked at the end.

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to [foundkeep.app/signup](https://foundkeep.app/signup).
2. Sign in with your provider of choice.
3. You'll land on your [dashboard](https://foundkeep.app/dashboard) — this is your
   library. It starts empty. That's about to change.

Every account starts on the Free plan. If you're here through the friends beta,
you already have Pro. See [Plans & storage](/plans-and-storage/) for what's included.

## 2. Pick a place to save from

FoundKeep meets you where you already are. Pick whichever fits how you work — you
can add the others later:

- **Browser extension** (Chrome, Edge, Brave) — the fastest way to save links,
  articles, and highlights while you read.
- **iOS app** — save from any app with the Share sheet, and jot notes in-app.
- **Android** — a signed beta APK from [foundkeep.app/beta](https://foundkeep.app/beta).
- **Web dashboard** — paste a URL, drop in files, or write a note directly.

Full step-by-step for each is in [Save from anywhere](/save-from-anywhere/).

## 3. Save your first thing

The quickest first save is a link you care about:

1. Install the [browser extension](https://foundkeep.app/beta) and pin it.
2. Open a page you'd like to keep.
3. Click the FoundKeep icon. That's it — the page is saved.

No extension handy? Open your [dashboard](https://foundkeep.app/dashboard), paste
a URL into the add box, and save. You can also write a note or drop a file the
same way.

## 4. Watch it get organized

Once something is saved, FoundKeep can do the filing for you. With AI processing
on, a new save gets:

- a short **summary**,
- suggested **tags**,
- a **category**,
- and, for images, **text pulled out with OCR** so it's searchable.

AI processing is available on every plan during early access. It's **consent-gated**
— you turn it on in settings, and you can re-run it on any single item later. See
[Privacy](/privacy/) for exactly what that means.

## 5. Find it again

This is the whole point. From your dashboard:

1. Start typing in **search** — it looks across titles, notes, summaries, and text
   found inside images.
2. Narrow with **filters** by type, status, or category.
3. Open a save to read its summary, add your own tags, or link it to related saves.

More on this in [Search & filters](/search-and-filters/).
