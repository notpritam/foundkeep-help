**Who it's for:** people who work with AI assistants — Claude, Codex — and want them
to draw on what *they've* actually read and kept, not just the open web.

Your FoundKeep library is a record of what matters to you. Connect an agent, and
that library becomes a tool the agent can read, search, and organize on your
behalf. It's a second brain that your AI can use.

<AgentMcpDiagram />

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. Save broadly. The more of your reading, notes, and references live in
   FoundKeep, the more useful your agent's memory is. See
   [Save from anywhere](/save-from-anywhere/).
2. Turn on [AI processing](/privacy/) so everything is summarized and searchable —
   that's what your agent searches over.
3. Connect your agent at [foundkeep.app/connect](https://foundkeep.app/connect).
   Full steps are in [Connect Claude & Codex](/connect-claude-codex/).

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="No token to copy">
You approve the connection with a browser sign-in, and you can revoke it any time
in **Settings → Agent connections**.
</Aside>

## The workflow

<Steps>

1. **Keep saving as usual.** Your library grows through normal use.
2. **Ask your agent to use it.** Once connected, ask Claude or Codex to search your
   library, pull the relevant saves, and work from them — "find what I saved about
   onboarding and draft an outline", "summarize my sleep-research folder".
3. **Let it organize.** The connection lets your agent help *tend* the library too —
   tagging, grouping, and linking — not just read it.

</Steps>

## Why it's different

- The agent works from **what you chose to keep**, so its answers are grounded in
  your sources, not guesses.
- Preserved [tweets and threads](/usecase-keep-tweets-forever/), readable article
  text, and OCR'd images are all fair game for the agent to search.

<Aside type="caution" title="It can't delete">
An agent can read, search, create, and organize — but removing a save is
owner-only. Nothing leaves your library without you.
</Aside>
