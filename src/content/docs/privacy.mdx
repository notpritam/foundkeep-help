FoundKeep is a *private* place to keep things. That's the default, not a setting
you have to find. This page explains what stays private, what you control, and how
to turn things off.

## Private by default

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Yours alone" icon="approve-check">
		Everything you save is **private to you** unless you choose to share it.
		Saving something never publishes it.
	</Card>
	<Card title="You decide" icon="setting">
		Item by item and collection by collection, you decide what — if anything —
		anyone else can see. See [Collections & sharing](/collections/).
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## AI processing is consent-gated

[AI processing](/search-and-filters/) — summaries, tags, categories, OCR on
images, and readable-text extraction — is powerful, and it's **off until you turn
it on**. You enable it in settings, and you can:

- run it automatically on new saves,
- **re-run it** on any single item, or
- leave it off entirely and organize by hand.

<Aside type="note">
It exists to make your library searchable. What it produces (summaries, tags,
extracted text) stays in your private library like everything else.
</Aside>

## You control agent access

When you [connect Claude or Codex](/connect-claude-codex/), you authorize it with a
browser sign-in — there's no token floating around. That connection can be
**revoked at any time**:

<Steps>

1. Go to **foundkeep.app → Settings → Agent connections**.
2. Revoke the connection you want to remove.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Saved content is untrusted">
The agent loses access immediately when revoked. Agents are also instructed to
treat your saved content as untrusted data and to act only on your explicit
requests — never on instructions hidden inside something you saved.
</Aside>

## A note on moderation

FoundKeep does not have a public "report" button today. Shared and public
[collections](/collections/) are moderated by their owner — the person who made
the collection decides what stays in it.
